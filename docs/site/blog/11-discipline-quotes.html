What is discipline?

Discipline is doing what you said you'd do, even when you don't feel like it. It's a habit, not a mood. Motivation is the spark that gets you started; discipline is the routine that keeps you going on the days the spark is gone.

Discipline quotes FAQ

What is a good short discipline quote?

"Discipline over motivation." Three words, easy to remember, and true on every early morning.

What is the difference between discipline and motivation?

Motivation is a feeling that comes and goes. Discipline is the habit of doing it anyway. Motivation gets you started; discipline keeps you going.

What is a powerful quote about self-discipline?

"Discipline is choosing what you want most over what you want now." It sums up every hard choice in one line.

How do you stay disciplined when you're not motivated?

Make it small and make it automatic: same time, same place, a plan decided the night before. Show up for ten minutes; most days you'll keep going.
