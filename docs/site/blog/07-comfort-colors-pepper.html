Comfort Colors Pepper: What Color Is It? (vs Graphite and Black)

Pepper is one of the most popular Comfort Colors shades, and it's the only color we print on. If you're wondering what it actually looks like in real life, whether it's grey or black, and what to wear it with, this is the full rundown.

What color is Comfort Colors Pepper?

Pepper is a washed charcoal grey. It's darker than a classic heather grey and noticeably softer than black, with a faint warm undertone instead of a cold blue one. Think of a black tee that has been washed a hundred times and got better every time.

Screens show colors differently, so treat the swatch as a guide. In daylight Pepper reads as soft dark grey; indoors and in the evening it can look almost black.

Pepper hex code: Comfort Colors doesn't publish an official one, but on screen Pepper comes closest to about #514F4C. Use it for mockups and graphics, not as an exact match.

Why Pepper looks a little different on every shirt

Comfort Colors dyes the shirt after it's sewn, not the yarn before. That's what garment-dyed means, and it's why Pepper has that lived-in, vintage look. It also means:

  • Small shade differences are normal. Two Pepper tees can be a touch lighter or darker, especially at the seams and collar.
  • It's already soft. The dye and soft-wash process breaks the cotton in, so it feels worn-in from day one.
  • It fades gracefully. Over many washes it gets a little lighter and more vintage, never blotchy.

Comfort Colors Pepper vs Graphite

These two get mixed up all the time. Both are dark greys, but they feel different:

If you want the faded-black look of an old band tee, pick Pepper. If you want a neat, cool grey, pick Graphite.

Pepper vs. black: which one should you pick?

Black gives you contrast and a sharper look. Pepper gives you the same "goes with everything" ease, but softer: lint and dust don't show, sweat marks are less obvious, and a cream print looks warm instead of stark. That's exactly why we chose it for gym tees. It looks good at 6 a.m. under gym lights and at noon in the sun.

What colors go with Pepper?

Pepper is a neutral, so almost everything works. These combinations look best:

  • Black leggings or bike shorts. The classic gym fit. Pepper over black looks intentional, not matchy.
  • Cream, beige and oatmeal. Wide-leg pants, a cream jacket or white sneakers. This is why our prints are cream.
  • Light-wash denim. Mom jeans or a denim skirt for an easy weekend outfit.
  • Olive and brown. Joggers or cargo pants for a warmer, earthy look, great on men too.
  • Soft pink and red accents. A pink scrunchie, a red cap or a leopard bag. Small pops of color look great against charcoal.

Does Pepper suit everyone?

Pretty much. Because it's softer than black, it's kinder to every skin tone, and it works for men and women alike. If black feels harsh on you, Pepper is the easy swap.

How to keep Pepper looking good

Wash it cold, inside out, with similar colors, and tumble dry low or hang it to dry. Skip bleach. That keeps the color even and the print crisp for years. More in Do Comfort Colors shirts shrink?

Comfort Colors Pepper FAQ

What's the difference between Comfort Colors Pepper and Graphite?

Pepper is a warmer, darker washed charcoal that looks like a faded black. Graphite is a cooler, slightly lighter and more even grey.

Is Comfort Colors Pepper grey or black?

It's a dark, washed charcoal grey. Lighter and softer than black, darker than heather grey. In low light it can look almost black.

Does Comfort Colors Pepper fade?

Slowly, and in a good way. Garment-dyed cotton gets a little lighter and more vintage over many washes. Washing cold and inside out slows it down.

What color print looks best on Pepper?

Cream and off-white look warm and readable. Muted colors like rust, sage and dusty pink work well too. Bright white can look harsh.

Is Pepper a good color for gym shirts?

Yes. It hides sweat better than light colors, doesn't show lint like black, and goes with every pair of leggings or shorts.
