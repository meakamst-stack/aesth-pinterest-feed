Plus Size Workout Outfits: 7 Gym Fits With an Oversized Tee (Up to 4XL)

The best workout outfit is the one you forget you're wearing. No tugging, no riding up, no checking the mirror between sets. For a lot of women that's an oversized tee over leggings or shorts. Here are seven plus size workout outfits built around one heavyweight tee, what to look for in plus size gym clothes, and how to pick your size.

1. Leg day: oversized tee + high-waisted leggings

The classic. A tee one size up over black high-waisted leggings and flat shoes. It covers you while you squat and hinge, and the back print does the talking. Back print: Confidence Is My Pre-Workout.

2. Dumbbell day: tee + longer biker shorts

Go for biker shorts with a 6 to 8 inch inseam so they stay in place on lunges and step-ups. Front-tuck the tee if it gets in the way on rows. Back print: Unbothered. Lifting.

3. Cardio day: side knot + shorts or capris

Tie the tee in a small knot at the hip so it doesn't flap on the treadmill, bike or stair climber. Pair with shorts or capri leggings and cushioned shoes.

4. Cold gym: tee over a long sleeve

A fitted long sleeve under the tee, or a zip hoodie over it. Take a layer off when you're warm; the tee is still the outfit.

5. Gym buddy: matching tees

Training with a friend makes it easier to show up. Wear tees that go together, like two Lifting Girls Club tees, or one each of Strong Girl Club and Strong Girl Energy.

6. The coffee run after: half-tuck + wide-leg jeans

Same tee, half-tucked into high-waisted wide-leg jeans, white sneakers, iced coffee. The front tuck gives you a waist without losing the relaxed fit. Back print: Pretty Strong.

7. Rest day: tee + joggers

Your oversized tee with soft joggers and clean sneakers. Rest days count too.

Plus size workout outfit FAQ

What should a plus size woman wear to the gym?

Whatever lets you move without thinking about it: high-waisted leggings or longer biker shorts, a supportive sports bra, flat shoes and a loose heavyweight tee.

Are oversized shirts good for plus size gym outfits?

Yes. A heavyweight oversized tee drapes instead of clinging, doesn't ride up when you squat or reach overhead, and works outside the gym too.

How do I stop my workout shorts from riding up?

Choose biker shorts with a longer inseam (6 to 8 inches) and a wide, high waistband, or switch to capri leggings.
