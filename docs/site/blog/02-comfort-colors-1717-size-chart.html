Does Comfort Colors run big or small?

Comfort Colors runs true to size with a relaxed, boxy cut. It's a unisex tee, so compared with a fitted women's tee it feels a size bigger; compared with a regular men's tee it fits about the same, just a little roomier through the body. The heavy, garment-dyed cotton hangs straight down instead of clinging, which makes it look bigger than the numbers suggest.

How to size up: the size ladder

Start from the size you'd normally wear in a women's tee, then go up by the look you're after:

How many sizes upThe fit
Your usual sizeRelaxed: roomy through the body, hem at the hip
1 size upOversized: dropped shoulders, sleeves to the elbow
2 sizes upPump cover: the big slouchy look in our photos
3 sizes upT-shirt dress: wear it on its own or over biker shorts

Most women at AESTH. wear one or two sizes up. If you're between sizes, go up: this tee is meant to hang loose. Not sure what a pump cover is? We explain it here.

The easiest way to pick: measure a tee you love

Take a tee that already fits the way you want. Lay it flat, measure armpit to armpit and compare it to the chest column above. That beats guessing from your usual size every time.

Do Comfort Colors shirts shrink?

A little. (Full answer and washing tips: do Comfort Colors shirts shrink?) The 1717 is 100% ring-spun cotton and garment-dyed, so most of the shrink already happened in the dye process. Expect up to about half a size in the wash, mostly in length, if you tumble dry hot. To keep it the way it came:

  • wash cold, inside out
  • tumble dry low, or hang to dry
  • skip bleach and high heat

If you want to play it safe, size up one more and let it shrink into place.

Why the 1717 for a pump cover?

It's heavyweight (around 6.1 oz) so it drapes instead of clinging, garment-dyed so it feels broken-in from day one, and the back is a big flat canvas for the print. Every AESTH. tee is printed on the back only, in cream ink, with nothing on the front.
