Gifts for Men Who Work Out: 15 Gym Gifts for Men He'll Actually Use

Buying for a guy who lives at the gym is harder than it looks. He already has a shaker, he already has headphones, and he will not tell you what he wants. So here are fifteen fitness gifts for men who work out, sorted by the kind of lifter he is, from tees with a message to the gear he keeps forgetting to buy for himself.

For the dad who still lifts

  1. A tee that says it for him. Dad Strength (a wrench crossed with a loaded dumbbell, "built different"), Strong Dad Club or, for the dad with a sense of humor, Dad Bod Under Construction. He'll wear it to the gym, to the hardware store and to every barbecue after.
  2. Adjustable dumbbells for the garage. For the dad who trains at home before everyone wakes up. Pick a set that changes weight with a dial so it doesn't eat the whole garage.
  3. A good pair of training shoes. Flat, stable soles for squats and deadlifts. Running shoes are not lifting shoes, and he probably knows it.

Gifts for gym bros

  1. An oversized pump cover. Every gym bro owns one, and they all wear out. Strong Guy Club (the matching partner to our Strong Girl Club tee) or Iron Therapy, for the guy who calls the weight room his therapist.
  2. Lifting straps. Cheap, small and he'll use them every pull day. Cotton or nylon both work; leather lasts longest.
  3. A leather lifting belt. The gift for the serious lifter. Ask his waist size discreetly, or pick a lever belt with a little room.

For the funny one

  1. A tee that makes the gym laugh. Will Lift for Tacos (a tiny taco pressing a barbell) is the one that gets comments between sets.
  2. A gym towel with a joke on it. Something he can throw over the bench and that nobody else will take.

Gym gifts for your boyfriend or husband

  1. The one that makes it about you. Her Favorite Heavy Lifter, with a very strong bear on the back. Wear your own tee next to him and it's a matching gym date.
  2. A couples class or a month at a better gym. Training together is the gift that keeps going.

For the disciplined one

  1. A quiet reminder on his back. Discipline Over Motivation or Trust the Process. Clean type, no cartoon, for the guy who trains at 5 a.m. without talking about it.
  2. A training journal. A paper log for sets, reps and PRs. Lifters who write it down make more progress.

Workout gifts for recovery

  1. A massage gun or a foam roller. His legs will thank you the day after leg day.
  2. A big insulated water bottle. The kind that keeps water cold through a two-hour session.

A small extra

  1. Chalk, a box of protein bars and gym socks. Small things every lifter runs out of. Perfect next to a bigger gift.

Gym gift FAQ

What do you get a guy who loves the gym?

Something he'll use every week: a heavyweight gym tee with a message, lifting straps, a lifting belt or a massage gun. Skip the shaker; he already has five.

What is the 5 gift rule?

Something he wants, something he needs, something to wear, something to read and something to do. For a lifter: a lifting belt, straps, a gym tee, a training journal and a class or a month at a better gym.

What is a good gym gift for a boyfriend?

A tee that's about the two of you, like Her Favorite Heavy Lifter, plus a training session together. Shared gym time beats another gadget.

How much should you spend on a gym gift?

It doesn't have to be much. Straps, chalk and a gym tee are all under $40; a belt or massage gun is the bigger gift.
