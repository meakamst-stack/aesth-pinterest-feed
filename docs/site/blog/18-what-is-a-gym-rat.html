What Is a Gym Rat? Meaning, Signs and the Gym Rat Uniform

You've seen the term in captions, on TikTok and maybe on a friend who's "just stopping by the gym" for the third time today. So what is a gym rat, exactly? Here's the meaning, where the name comes from, the signs you might be one, and the unofficial gym rat uniform.

Gym rat meaning

A gym rat is someone who spends a lot of time at the gym and loves it. They train most days, know the staff by name, have a favorite squat rack and plan the rest of their day around their workout. Today it's mostly a compliment, or a proud self-label: "I'm such a gym rat."

Where does "gym rat" come from?

It follows an old pattern in American slang: a "rat" is someone who's always hanging around one place, like a mall rat or a rink rat. Put someone at the gym every day and they become a gym rat. Social media turned it from a mild tease into an identity, with its own memes, outfits and, yes, merch.

12 signs you're a gym rat

  1. Your gym bag never gets unpacked. It just gets refilled.
  2. You know the gym's busy hours better than your own schedule.
  3. Rest day feels like a punishment.
  4. You've had "your" squat rack taken and felt personally attacked.
  5. You plan vacations around whether the hotel has a gym.
  6. Half your wardrobe is oversized tees and the other half is leggings or shorts.
  7. You own more shakers than mugs.
  8. Your camera roll is 40% mirror selfies and progress pics.
  9. You say "just one more set" and mean five.
  10. You know exactly which dumbbells are missing from the rack.
  11. Your friends text you "at the gym again?" and you are.
  12. You read this list nodding.

The gym rat uniform

Gym rats don't overthink outfits; they wear what works every single day:

  • An oversized heavyweight tee. The pump cover, ideally with something to say on the back: Iron Therapy, Lifting Is My Therapy or In My Lifting Era.
  • Black leggings or training shorts. Always clean, always a backup in the bag.
  • Flat lifting shoes and crew socks.
  • A shaker, usually with something in it. Eat Your Protein is basically the gym rat's motto.
  • Headphones and straps. The rest is in the gym bag essentials.

Is being a gym rat a good thing?

Mostly, yes: consistent training, a routine and a place that feels like yours. The best gym rats also take their rest days seriously, because muscles grow when you recover, not only when you lift.

Gym rat FAQ

What does gym rat mean?

A gym rat is someone who goes to the gym very often and loves it. It's usually meant as a compliment or used as a proud self-description.

Is "gym rat" an insult?

Rarely. It started as light teasing, but most people now use it proudly.

What is the difference between a gym rat and a gym bunny?

A gym rat is all about training and being at the gym a lot. A gym bunny usually also enjoys the social and style side of the gym.

What do gym rats wear?

Oversized heavyweight tees, leggings or training shorts, flat lifting shoes, crew socks, headphones and a shaker.
