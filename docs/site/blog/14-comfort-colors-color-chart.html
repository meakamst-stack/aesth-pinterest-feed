Comfort Colors Color Chart: The Most Popular 1717 Colors, Explained

Comfort Colors makes the 1717 heavyweight tee in more than 60 shades, and they all have that soft, washed, garment-dyed look. This color chart groups the most popular ones by family, so you can see at a glance which neutrals, pastels, earth tones and brights are out there, and which one fits what you want to wear.

Good to know before you scroll: the swatches below are approximate screen colors, not official codes. Comfort Colors doesn't publish hex values, screens differ, and because every shirt is dyed after it's sewn, the real color varies a little from tee to tee. Retailer color lists for 2026 add Dusk (a muted mauve), Rose Quartz (a soft pink-lilac) and Neon Cantaloupe, and list Lagoon Blue under the shorter name Lagoon. Colors come and go each season, so if you need one specific shade, check that it's still in stock before you plan around it.

Why Comfort Colors look different from other tees

  • Garment-dyed. The tee is sewn first and dyed after, so seams and collars take color slightly differently. That's where the vintage look comes from.
  • Soft-washed. The shirts are washed after dyeing, which softens the cotton and mutes the color a little.
  • Pre-shrunk. The dye process also shrinks the cotton, so they barely shrink at home.
  • Heavyweight. About 6.1 oz cotton, so even light colors don't go see-through.

Comfort Colors color chart FAQ

How many colors does Comfort Colors have?

The 1717 heavyweight tee comes in more than 60 colors. The exact lineup changes a little every season as shades are added and retired.

What is the most popular Comfort Colors color?

Neutrals sell the most: Pepper, Sandstone, Ivory, Black and Graphite show up on nearly every shop's best-seller list, with Blue Jean and Chalky Mint close behind.

Does Comfort Colors have hex codes?

Not official ones. Garment dyeing makes every shirt slightly different, so any hex code (including the swatches on this page) is an on-screen approximation.

Why does my Comfort Colors shirt look different from the photo?

Screens show color differently, and garment-dyed tees vary a little from batch to batch. A shade lighter or darker than the photo is normal.
