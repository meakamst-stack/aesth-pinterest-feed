  • On your back: the quote you'll actually see most is the one on the person in front of you at the squat rack. Ours are on heavyweight pump cover tees.

FAQ

What to caption a gym pic?

Keep it short: "Built, not bought.", "PR day.", "Quiet work, loud results." or "Unbothered. Lifting." Under five words reads best under a photo.

What is the best caption for exercise?

One that sounds like you. Funny if you're the funny one ("Sleep is my cardio"), short and steady if you're the disciplined one ("5 a.m. club").

What is a good discipline quote?

"Discipline is choosing what you want most over what you want now." Or the short version: "Discipline over motivation."

What is a good short gym quote?

"Discipline over motivation", "Lift anyway" and "One more rep" are short, easy to remember and work as captions too.

What's the difference between motivation and discipline?

Motivation is a feeling that comes and goes. Discipline is the habit of showing up anyway. Most lifters find discipline lasts longer.
