Gym Crush: Is It Normal, Signs They Like You and How to Say Hi

Same time, same gym, same person at the rack next to you. Suddenly you're doing your hair for leg day. Welcome to the gym crush. Here's why it happens, the signs they might like you back, how to say hi without making it weird, and what never to do.

Is it normal to have a gym crush?

Completely. You see the same people at the same time every week, you share a routine, and everyone's in a good mood after a workout. That's the perfect recipe for a crush. Most regulars have had one; plenty of couples met between sets.

Signs your gym crush might like you back

None of these are proof on their own, but a few together are a good sign:

  • They take their headphones out when you're nearby.
  • They start showing up at your usual time.
  • They smile or nod every time you make eye contact.
  • They ask you something they could easily figure out ("Are you using this?", "How many sets left?").
  • They offer a spot or say "nice set".
  • They pick the station right next to you when the gym is half empty.

How to talk to your gym crush (without being awkward)

  1. Pick the right moment. Between sets, while they're resting or leaving. Never mid-set.
  2. Check the headphones. Both in and eyes down means focus mode. Wait for another day.
  3. Start small. "Are you using this?" or "Can I work in?" is the most natural opener in any gym. Not sure what that means? See our gym etiquette guide.
  4. Compliment the effort, not the body. "That was a strong set" lands a lot better than anything about how they look.
  5. Keep it short. A minute of chat, then back to your workout. Leave them wanting the next conversation.
  6. Make the next step easy. "Want to grab a coffee after the gym sometime?" is low pressure.
  7. Take no gracefully. If they're not interested, say "no worries" and keep training like normal. The gym should stay comfortable for both of you.

What not to do

  • Don't stare. A glance is fine. Watching every set is not.
  • Don't film them. Ever. Not even as a joke.
  • Don't give unsolicited form advice as an opener.
  • Don't follow them from station to station or wait outside the locker room.
  • Don't make it the reason you go. Train for you; the crush is a bonus.

Gym crush FAQ

What is a gym crush?

A crush on someone you regularly see at the gym. Usually a regular who trains at the same time as you.

How do you know if your gym crush likes you?

Look for a few signs together: they take their headphones out near you, smile when you make eye contact, start conversations and pick the spot next to you.

Is it okay to ask someone out at the gym?

Yes, if you do it respectfully: between sets, short and low pressure, and you accept a no without making things awkward.

How do you deal with a gym crush?

Keep training for yourself, say hi when the moment is right, and enjoy it. Worst case, you've got extra motivation to show up.
